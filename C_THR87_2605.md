## Exam Structure & Official Details

To optimize your preparation, you must understand the exact parameters of the exam:
*   **Format:** Scenario-Based Assessment (SyBA) (1 activity)
*   **Duration:** 2 hours (120 minutes)
*   **Passing Score:** 60%
*   **Target Audience:** SAP SuccessFactors consultants, HR compensation professionals, and implementation partners

## Core Exam Competencies & Syllabus Focus

The C_THR87_2605 exam tests your proficiency across several critical domains of compensation management. Focus your study efforts on these key functional areas:

*   **Bonus Planning & Incentive Compensation:** Structuring complex variable pay programs based on organizational goals and performance metrics.
*   **Performance-Based Payouts:** Configuring payout curves, business goal weights, and individual performance multipliers.
*   **Program Setup & Employee Data:** Managing eligibility rules, employee history data, and routing maps.
*   **Form Setup & Management:** Designing variable pay worksheets, defining custom fields, and managing the compensation cycle.
*   **Integration & Advanced Topics:** Seamlessly integrating Variable Pay with SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central and configuring the Total Compensation Plan Template.

## Strategic Approach for Scenario-Based Questions

The modern C_THR87_2605 exam relies heavily on scenario-based questions that simulate real enterprise contexts.

1.  **Analyze Business Requirements:** You will be presented with a hypothetical client situation. Focus on understanding the business logic and compensation rules before looking at the configuration options.
2.  **Evaluate Configuration Impact:** Do not just memorize steps; understand why a specific configuration choice is made and how it impacts the downstream variable pay calculation.
3.  **Cross-Domain Integration:** Recognize that decisions in employee data management directly affect bonus plan execution. Practice evaluating dependencies between different system modules.
