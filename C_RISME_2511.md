## Core Exam Competencies

The C_RISME_2511 exam tests your proficiency across several critical domains of cloud transformation. Focus your study efforts on these key areas:

*   **SAP Activate for RISE with SAP:** Understanding the phases, deliverables, and tasks specific to private and public cloud deployments.
*   **Business Process Intelligence (BPI):** Utilizing SAP Signavio for process discovery, analysis, and continuous improvement.
*   **Cloud Architecture & Infrastructure:** Navigating the technical foundations, system provisioning, and lifecycle management of SAP S/4HANA Cloud.
*   **Migration & Integration:** Strategies for migrating legacy systems and integrating SAP BTP (Business Technology Platform) to maintain a clean core.
