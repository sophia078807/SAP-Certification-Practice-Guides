# SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition, Service Study Guide (C_TS470)

The C_TS470 certification shows that you can set up and run service processes inside SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition. Like other updated SAP exams, this is no longer a standard multiple-choice test. It is now a System-Based Assessment, which means you log into a live system and complete real configuration and transaction tasks.

If you want to pass on your first try, you need hands-on practice with how service data, orders, and repairs connect. You can go through our full [SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition, Service Study Guide](https://theexamquestions.com/blog/c-ts470-sap-exam-guide) to review the exact exam scenarios and check where your weak spots are before booking your test date.

## Exam facts you need to know

Here is how the C_TS470 exam is currently set up:

* **Exam Code:** C_TS470
* **Format:** System-Based Assessment (1 activity)
* **Time Limit:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
* **Passing Score:** 71%
* **Language:** English
* **Who should take it:** SAP consultants, business users, and project team members working on S/4HANA service setups. You do not strictly need past project experience, though system practice is a must.

## Official topic weights

Because the passing score is 71%, you cannot afford to skip the heavy sections. Organizational and master data alone make up more than a third of the test:

| Topic Area | Weight | What you need to know |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organizational and Master Data** | 31% - 40% | Service organizational models, business partners, employees, products, product bundles, functional locations, equipment, serial numbers, bills of material (BOMs), measuring points, and counters. |
| **General Configuration of Service Processing** | 21% - 30% | Transaction types, item categories and determination, copying control, pricing setup, and integrations with Controlling (CO) and the Cross-Application Time Sheet (CATS). |
| **On-Site Service Processing** | 11% - 20% | Handling service requests, quotations, service orders, confirmations, and service billing from start to finish, including field service and advanced execution steps. |
| **In-House Service Processing** | 11% - 20% | Setting up and running in-house repairs, managing service contracts, and scheduling recurring services using order templates, task lists, and maintenance plans. |

## How to prepare for the live system test

Reading slides will not be enough for a 3-hour system test. Keep your study routine practical:

1. **Get master data right first:** Almost every service order or repair flow fails if the organizational structure or technical objects (like equipment and functional locations) are set up wrong. Practice building these cleanly from scratch.
2. **Trace the full service chain:** Run through the whole cycle from a customer service request to a quotation, service order, confirmation, and final billing until you can do it without looking up the steps.
3. **Check your results in the system:** In a System-Based Assessment, finishing a step is only half the job. Make sure you know how to check the document flow, status changes, and billing logs to prove your configuration actually worked.
4. **Practice under a timer:** Three hours sounds like plenty of time, but hunting through customizing menus eats up minutes fast. Practice your configuration paths so you know where transaction types, item categories, and copying control live.
