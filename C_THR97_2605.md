## Core Exam Objectives (Syllabus Breakdown)

This module validates your ability to implement and manage the SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting suite. Focus your studies on these primary architecture components:

*   **Candidate Relationship Management (CRM):** Managing talent pools, data capture forms, and email campaigns.
*   **Applicant Tracking System (ATS):** Navigating the candidate pipeline, application workflows, and offer approvals.
*   **Interview Scheduling:** Configuring Interview Central, setting up assessments, and integrating Outlook/calendars.
*   **Career Site Builder (CSB):** Designing responsive, branded candidate portals and landing pages.
*   **Job Requisitions:** Configuring approval routing, job profiles, and external job board distribution.
