# C_IBP_2608 Study Guide: SAP Integrated Business Planning

The SAP Certified Associate - SAP Integrated Business Planning (IBP) for Supply Chain certification validates your core skills in supply chain planning. SAP has recently shifted to performance-based testing for 2026, meaning this exam is no longer multiple-choice. It is now a **System-Based Assessment (SyBA)** where you must carry out tasks in a live system context and identify the evidence that confirms it worked.

This guide breaks down the essential topics and system mechanics you need to master to pass the C_IBP_2608 exam.

## Exam Structure & Official Domain Weightage

Under the new 2026 format, the exam parameters have fundamentally changed:
*   **Format:** System-Based Assessment (SyBA) (1 activity)
*   **Duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
*   **Passing Score:** 90%

Understanding the exact weightage is critical for prioritizing your hands-on practice:

| Domain Area | Approximate Weight | Core Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Demand** | 54% | Supports modern demand planning, forecasting, and demand changes. |
| **Configuration** | 21% – 30% | Setting up systems, basic setup of IBP features, and data management. |
| **Supply** | 21% – 30% | Supply network planning, response, and related processes. |

## System-Based Assessment Strategy

In a SyBA exam, recall gets you partway, but fluency finishes the job. You will be asked to perform configurations or execution tasks and prove they succeeded.

1. **Verify Your Work:** The hard part of the exam is knowing which signal in the system tells you the step actually succeeded. Practice identifying the exact posting that landed or the status that changed.
2. **Master the Add-in:** Ensure you are fluent in using the SAP IBP Add-in for Microsoft Excel, as this is a primary tool for planners to review business data and planning views.
3. **Understand Data Flow:** Learn how products, locations, and time profiles affect planning results, because poor master data leads to wrong plans during system tasks.
