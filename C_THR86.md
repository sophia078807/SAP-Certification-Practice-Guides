# C_THR86: Active-Recall Flashcards for the 2-Hour Scenario Exam

Passing the SAP SuccessFactors Compensation certification takes more than reading through slides. Because SAP runs this test as a 2-hour Scenario-Based Assessment, you have to pull configuration details, business rules, and permission logic from memory under time pressure. You do not get a list of multiple-choice hints to jog your memory.

Using active-recall flashcards is one of the fastest ways to lock in these system rules before test day. Instead of passively re-reading notes, flashcards force your brain to retrieve the exact answer- which is the exact skill you need during a live scenario. If you want a ready-made set of study drills, you can practice with our [C_THR86](https://theexamquestions.com/course/c-thr86-sap-exam-questions) flashcards and scenario questions to spot your weak areas early.

## Exam facts to keep in mind

Before you start your flashcard drills, make sure you know what the test looks like:

* **Exam Code:** C_THR86
* **Product:** SAP SuccessFactors Compensation
* **Format:** Scenario-Based Assessment (1 activity)
* **Time Limit:** 2 hours (120 minutes)
* **Passing Score:** 60%

## High-value flashcard topics to build and drill

Group your flashcard decks by the real tasks you will face in the scenario. Focus heavily on these areas:

* **Platform Foundations & Admin Setup:** Drill the difference between what happens in Provisioning versus Admin Center, along with Role-Based Permissions (RBP), proxy management, and Metadata Framework (MDF) business rules.
* **Implementation Kickoff:** Test yourself on connecting the Data Type Definition (DTD) to the compensation XML plan and assigning the right administrator permissions before launch.
* **Data Integration & Grouping:** Practice recalling how employee data flows into the compensation module and how grouping rules control which employees appear on specific worksheets.
* **Plans, Route Maps & Worksheets:** Use cards to lock in how approval route maps work, how to link a plan template to Employee Central, and how managers use salary and stock sheets during a review cycle.
* **Budgets, Eligibility & Guidelines:** Drill the logic behind budget caps, eligibility rules, merit guidelines, and connecting performance rating sources to pay increases.
* **Publishing to Employee Central & Promotions:** Make sure you can recall the exact steps for writing approved pay changes and promotions back into Employee Central without breaking the employee record.
* **Reports & Compensation Statements:** Review standard reporting tools and the setup needed to generate final compensation statements for employees.

## How to use flashcards for a 2-hour scenario exam

Flashcards only work if you use them the right way. Keep your sessions short and focused:

1. **Test the "why," not just the name:** Write questions that ask *what happens* when a setting is turned on, or *which tool* fixes a broken data flow, rather than simple vocabulary definitions.
2. **Say the answer out loud:** Force yourself to explain the step in plain words before flipping the card. If you cannot explain it clearly, put the card back in the "review again" pile.
3. **Mix your decks:** In the real 2-hour exam, a single scenario can jump from permissions to budgets to Employee Central publishing. Shuffle your topic decks together during your final week so you can switch gears quickly.
